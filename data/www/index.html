    Closed-loop control

    The PID corrects in flow units through the calibration inverse, so Kp is dimensionless and Ki is per second. Defaults Kp 0.3, Ki 0.05 suit most pumps. Pulsatile (diaphragm) pumps: set the averaging window to several pump cycles (≥ 20 s at low speed).

    Hardware: drivers & pins

    Changes apply without recompiling once all channels are stopped. Illegal pins (flash 6-11, strapping 0/2/5/12/15, UART 1/3, input-only 34-39) and conflicts are rejected. A ULN2003 board can only drive a unipolar stepper (28BYJ-48); NEMA17 needs a STEP/DIR driver.

    General

    The watchdog stops runs when no browser has sent a heartbeat for this long, unless the run was started with “Keep running if disconnected”. Sensor FAULT, air-in-line and E-STOP still apply.

    Wi-Fi

    The ESP32 access point is always on (192.168.4.1). Campus Wi-Fi often blocks mDNS, isolates clients or needs WPA2-Enterprise; use the AP or a phone hotspot. The AP handles about 4 clients.
